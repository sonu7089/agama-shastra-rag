# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Participation in this project should be respectful, constructive, and professional. Contributors are expected to discuss ideas in good faith, accept technical feedback, and avoid personal attacks or dismissive behavior.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful in reviews and discussions.
- Focus criticism on code, documentation, or process.
- Assume incomplete work is usually a product of time constraints, not bad intent.
- Share sources and reasoning when making technical claims.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or intimidation
- Discriminatory language or exclusionary behavior
- Deliberate misrepresentation of project status, benchmarks, or results
- Publishing private credentials, personal data, or sensitive corpus material without permission

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation for behavior that violates this code of conduct.

## Reporting

If you need to report an issue, contact the repository maintainer privately through the contact channel listed on the repository profile.
